Final Boss: Thesis

A Study of Computer-Mediated Communication and Parasocial Intimacy in Otome Games

Final-year thesis · English Language & Literature

Explored how romance games make players feel genuinely close to characters, looking at the language characters use and how the app is designed.

How I did it

  • Qualitative linguistic analysis of in-game chat logs and interfaces from Mystic Messenger and Blooming Panic.
  • Questionnaire with 5 female players aged 18 to 26.
  • Thematic analysis, cross-checked across both data sources.

What I found

  • Characters use emojis, typos, and slang to sound human, and emojis soften awkward moments.
  • Public chatrooms make characters act performative, while private messages feel more intimate.
  • Simulated mistakes and personal attention made players feel the closeness was mutual and safe.
